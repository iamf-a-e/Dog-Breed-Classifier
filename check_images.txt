Questions regarding Uploaded Image Classification:

1. Did the three model architectures classify the breed of dog in Dog_01.jpg to be the same breed? If not, report the differences in the classifications.

Answer: Yes. All three architectures (resnet, alexnet and vgg) classified Dog_01.jpg as a papillon. None of them identified the true breed, which is a chihuahua.


2. Did each of the three model architectures classify the breed of dog in Dog_01.jpg to be the same breed of dog as that model architecture classified Dog_02.jpg? If not, report the differences in the classifications.

Answer: No, not for any model. Resnet and vgg both classified Dog_01.jpg as a papillon and the flipped Dog_02.jpg as a blenheim spaniel. Alexnet classified Dog_01.jpg as a papillon and Dog_02.jpg as a chihuahua, which is the correct breed, but it is still a different answer from the one it gave for Dog_01.jpg.


3. Did the three model architectures correctly classify Animal_Name_01.jpg and Object_Name_01.jpg to not be dogs? If not, report the misclassifications.

Answer: Not all of them. Resnet and vgg correctly classified both Black_panther_01.jpg and iPhone_01.jpg as not dogs: resnet said american black bear and cleaver/meat cleaver/chopper, and vgg said cougar/puma/mountain lion and mousetrap. Alexnet got the iPhone_01.jpg right as a not-dog (safety pin) but misclassified Black_panther_01.jpg as an irish water spaniel, which is a dog.


4. Based upon your answers for questions 1. - 3. above, select the model architecture that you feel did the best at classifying the four uploaded images. Describe why you selected that model architecture as the best on uploaded image classification.

Answer: I would pick vgg as the best. Resnet and vgg both scored 100% on dog vs. not-dog and gave the same dog labels, but vgg’s label for the panther (cougar, a big cat) is much closer to the true animal than resnet’s american black bear.
